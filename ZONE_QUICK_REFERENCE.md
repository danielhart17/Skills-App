# Zone-Based Shooting - Quick Reference Card

## 🎯 How It Works

### 1. Start Session
**Web**: Click "Start Session" button  
**iOS**: Tap "Start Session" button

### 2. Select Zone
**Web**: Click any zone on the court → Zone highlights blue  
**iOS**: Tap any zone on the court → Zone highlights blue

### 3. Record Shot
**Both**: Choose "✅ Made" or "❌ Miss"

### 4. Repeat
Keep selecting zones and recording shots

### 5. End Session
**Web**: Click "End Session" → View summary  
**iOS**: Tap "End Session" → View summary

---

## 🏀 The 11 Zones

| Zone | Location | Color | Type |
|------|----------|-------|------|
| **Left Corner** | Far left baseline | ⚫ Gray | Corner shots |
| **Left Wing** | Left near baseline | 🔵 Blue | Wing/elbow |
| **Restricted Area** | Center baseline/paint | 🔴 Red | Close range |
| **Right Wing** | Right near baseline | 🔴 Red | Wing/elbow |
| **Right Corner** | Far right baseline | ⚫ Gray | Corner shots |
| **Top of Key** | Above paint | 🔴 Red | Mid-range |
| **Free Throw** | FT circle | 🔴 Red | Free throws |
| **Left Mid** | Left mid-court | 🔴 Red | Mid-range |
| **Right Mid** | Right mid-court | 🔴 Red | Mid-range |
| **Left Baseline** | Left below FT | 🔴 Red | Baseline |
| **Right Baseline** | Right below FT | 🔴 Red | Baseline |

---

## 📊 What You See

### On Each Zone (After Shots)
```
4-8     ← Made shots - Total attempts
50%     ← Shooting percentage
```

### In Session Summary
- **Total Shots**: All attempts
- **Made Shots**: Successful shots
- **Accuracy**: Overall percentage
- **Duration**: Time elapsed
- **🏆 Best Zone**: Highest percentage zone

---

## 💡 Pro Tips

1. **Start with corners** - Easier shots to build confidence
2. **Practice weak zones** - Focus on low-percentage areas
3. **Balance your practice** - Try all zones each session
4. **Set goals** - Aim for 50%+ mid-range, 40%+ three-point
5. **Track progress** - Compare sessions over time

---

## ⚡ Quick Actions

### During Session
- **Undo**: Remove last shot (updates stats)
- **Pause** (Web only): Pause timer
- **End**: Stop and save session

### After Session
- **Save**: Keep session in history
- **Discard**: Delete session

## 🐛 Troubleshooting

**Court not showing?**  
→ Check image file exists: `half-court.png`

**Zones not clickable?**  
→ Make sure session is started (timer running)

**Stats not updating?**  
→ Verify you clicked "Made" or "Miss" after selecting zone

**Wrong zone selected?**  
→ Tap/click more precisely in center of zone

## 🎯 Practice Drills

### Drill 1: Corner Specialist
- 10 shots from each corner
- Goal: 40%+ from both corners

### Drill 2: Paint Domination
- 15 shots from paint zones
- Goal: 60%+ accuracy

### Drill 3: Around the World
- 2 shots from each zone (22 total)
- Goal: 50%+ overall

### Drill 4: Three-Point Challenge
- 5 shots from each 3PT zone (20 total)
- Goal: 35%+ accuracy

### Drill 5: Weak Zone Focus
- 20 shots from your worst zone
- Goal: Improve by 10%

---

## 📊 Tracking Progress

### Daily
- Complete 1-2 sessions
- Focus on 2-3 zones
- Track best zone

### Weekly
- Compare zone percentages
- Identify improvement areas
- Set new goals

### Monthly
- Review all sessions
- Calculate zone averages
- Celebrate progress! 🎉

## 🏆 Achievement Ideas

- **Sharpshooter**: 80%+ from any zone
- **Consistent**: 50%+ from all zones
- **Corner King**: 50%+ from both corners
- **Paint Master**: 70%+ from all paint zones
- **Three-Point Threat**: 40%+ from all 3PT zones
- **Perfect Session**: 100% from any zone (min 5 shots)
- **Century Club**: 100 total shots in one session
- **Marathon**: 30+ minute session
